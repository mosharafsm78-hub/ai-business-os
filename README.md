# Forge — AI Business OS

Forge is a visionary AI-powered entrepreneurship platform: a person chooses what kind of business they want, and an AI operating team helps research, source, brand, launch, market, support and optimize it.

## Current build

The repository contains a responsive, interactive product prototype covering the complete entrepreneur journey:

- AI Business Advisor
- Business opportunity discovery
- Product sourcing and procurement
- Brand studio
- Store launch center
- AI marketing center
- AI customer support
- Business analytics
- New-business onboarding
- Responsive entrepreneur workspace
- GitHub Pages deployment workflow

## Product principle

The entrepreneur makes the important choices. AI handles research, recommendations, creation, automation and day-to-day operating assistance. The platform company provides the supply/procurement layer behind the scenes.

## Prototype note

The current experience uses simulated local actions so the complete UX can be tested before production integrations are connected. Production work will replace these simulations with authentication, AI APIs, payments, supplier/procurement services, commerce, logistics and advertising APIs.

## Deployment

GitHub Pages deployment is configured through GitHub Actions.
