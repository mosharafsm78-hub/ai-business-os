# AI Business OS — Product Vision

## One-line promise

Start and operate a real business with an AI partner doing the work behind the scenes.

## Core principle

The entrepreneur makes strategic choices and owns the business. AI handles research, recommendations, creation, automation, and day-to-day operating assistance. The platform company provides the supply and execution infrastructure behind product sourcing.

## Entrepreneur journey

1. Discover a business opportunity.
2. Choose a recommended product/business model.
3. Review investment, pricing, margin, demand, and risk.
4. Place an inventory order through the platform.
5. AI creates the business identity and launch plan.
6. AI prepares the store and social presence.
7. AI creates and optimizes marketing.
8. AI handles customer conversations and routine operations.
9. Entrepreneur receives inventory and fulfills local orders.
10. AI continuously analyzes performance and recommends the next action.

## Design direction

Premium, confident, futuristic, human, and trustworthy. Avoid the generic purple AI-dashboard look. The interface should communicate that a real company is standing behind the AI.

## MVP principle

Build the experience first around one coherent journey: a new entrepreneur can answer a few questions, receive a business recommendation, inspect a product opportunity, and progress toward launching the business.
